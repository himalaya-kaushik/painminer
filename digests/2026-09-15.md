### 1. MoE efficiency gains hit a ceiling against dense models
Qwen3.6-27b (dense) matches the performance of Qwen3.6-35b-a3b (MoE with 3B active parameters). This challenges the assumption that scaling active parameters in MoE is the only path to quality, forcing a re-evaluation of inference cost versus quality trade-offs for production systems.
